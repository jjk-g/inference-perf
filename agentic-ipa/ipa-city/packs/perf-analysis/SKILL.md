# Perf Analysis Skill

This skill describes how to archive and index benchmark results in the Gas City Library.

## Archive Results
Instructions for archiving and indexing results.

### Process
1. **Prepare results**: Ensure benchmark results are in the expected format (JSON).
2. **Move to archive**: Place results in the `archive/` directory.
3. **Index in Library**: Update the library index or catalog to reflect the new results.
4. **Link to models**: Ensure results are associated with the correct model knowledge files.
